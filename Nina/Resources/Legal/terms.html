If you download Nina through the App Store, Apple’s applicable terms govern your App Store download and license.

Profiles and Crew

You can create a local profile with an email and password or use Sign in with Apple. Keep your device and sign-in details secure. Your journal stays on that iPhone.

Crew is an on-device community simulation. Comments, follows, reports and drafts are recorded in the app on that device. Reports hide content in your view. The call screen is a local preview with a waiting state. Nina does not send drafts or connect calls to other skaters.

Media and conduct

You keep your rights in the photos, clips, music and notes you add. Only add material you have the right to use. Get permission from people in your recordings when required. Follow the Crew Rules in the app and avoid threats, harassment, exploitation, unlawful material and sharing someone else’s private information without permission.

Skate safety

Skateboarding involves a risk of injury. Use protective gear, follow venue rules and local laws, and skate within your abilities. Check conditions and whether access is permitted before riding a spot. Use clips as skate references and make your own decisions on when and where to ride.

Grip and purchases

Where available, Grip can unlock optional journal covers in Nina. It has no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed for money. The Apple purchase sheet shows the price before you confirm. Apple handles billing and refund requests for App Store purchases.

Your choices

In Nina, open Crew, then Settings, and choose Delete profile to remove your local profile, journal, drafts, media and Grip balance. Deleting a profile does not request a refund from Apple.
