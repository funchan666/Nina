Terms of Use

Using Nina

Nina provides skate media, a practice journal, saved spots and skater profiles. Review these terms and the Crew Rules before creating a profile.

Media and safety

Only submit photos, recordings and music you have permission to use. Respect venue rules and the safety of people using a spot. Submissions stay in your own review queue.

Profiles and conversations

Profile actions and journal entries are available on this iPhone. Conversation drafts stay in Nina. The call screen checks permissions and shows a waiting state.

Grip

Grip unlocks optional journal covers. Use the price Apple shows before accepting a purchase. Grip is used within Nina and has no cash value.

Your choices

You can sign out, delete your profile, remove your submissions, hide content or block a skater in the app.
