Privacy Policy

Nina keeps your skate journal on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app saves, when it uses Apple’s services and what happens when you visit the official website.

On your iPhone

Nina saves your profile details, agreement date, spots, trick logs, saved lines and media drafts so you can open them again. Crew actions such as comments, follows, blocks and reports also remain on the device. Nina does not upload this content to a Nina server or sync it between devices.

Local email sign-in uses a one-way identifier derived from the email address. Nina stores a salted password verifier and session information in the iPhone Keychain. The password is used for local registration and sign-in and is not stored as plain text.

Photos, camera and microphone

Nina uses the iOS photo picker to import the photos and videos you select. When you use capture, Nina can ask to use the camera and, for video with sound, the microphone. If you open a skate call preview, Nina can ask for camera and microphone access for the local waiting screen. The preview is not transmitted.

Imported and captured copies stay in Nina on this iPhone. Use iOS Settings to change camera and microphone permissions at any time.

Apple services

With Sign in with Apple, Nina uses an Apple-provided identifier to open the matching local profile. On first authorization Nina may also receive the name you choose to share. Apple manages the sign-in authorization.

For in-app purchases, Apple handles payments. Nina uses product and transaction information from StoreKit to deliver Grip and prevent duplicate credit. Nina does not receive your payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Apple’s handling of sign-in and payment information.

Retention and deletion

Your profile content stays in Nina until you remove it. Open Crew, then Settings, and choose Delete profile to clear the local profile, its content and saved sign-in details. A minimal transaction or cleanup record may remain on the device to prevent the same transaction from being credited twice and to complete removal of local files.

Removing a Nina profile does not delete records held by Apple. Manage Sign in with Apple from your Apple Account settings.

Official website

The Nina website provides app information and these policies. Nina does not use the site to collect account details, serve ads or measure its audience. The hosting provider processes requests, including IP addresses and requested pages, to deliver the site. Its Cloudflare protection may run browser checks and set a security cookie to help distinguish automated traffic. The website cannot access the profile and journal stored in the app.
