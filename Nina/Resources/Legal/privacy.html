Privacy Policy

Profile and journal

Nina keeps your profile, practice history, comments, follows, reports and draft media in its app storage. Email entry uses a one-way identifier. The password field is used for entry validation and the password is discarded. Apple supplies a sign-in identifier and, on your first authorization, the name you choose to share.

Photo and audio access

You choose specific media with the iOS photo picker. Camera and microphone access is requested when you open capture or a waiting screen. Selected or captured files stay in Nina unless you share them separately. The front camera check is shown only on your screen.

External services

When media is available, the media host receives the network request needed to serve its images and videos. Apple handles Apple sign-in and StoreKit transactions.

Deletion and transactions

Delete Profile removes your profile, journal, draft media and associated interactions. A minimal transaction identifier may be kept to prevent granting the same Grip purchase twice.
