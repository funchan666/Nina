Crew Rules

Skate with respect

Respect spot access rules, give bystanders space and let others use the area. Do not encourage dangerous or illegal activity.

Content and conversation

Keep harassment, threats, hateful material, explicit sexual material, graphic violence, child exploitation, impersonation and spam out of Nina. Keep other people’s private information and faces out of a post unless they have agreed to share them. Credit people whose work you are authorized to post.

Use report and block

Use Report on a line, comment, profile or conversation to hide it and record your concern. Block cuts off contact in the app. Manage your blocked list in Settings.

Share carefully

Leave private addresses and identifying details out of shared spot notes. Only opt in to showing a practice summary in Crew when you want others to see it.
